Performance Analytics (The Sales Killer Feature)
This is what makes schools buy the product. Here's what we can build from the marks data:

For Parents & Students
📊 Subject Trend Chart — Line graph showing marks across exams (Unit 1 → Unit 2 → Mid-Term). "Is my child improving in Math?"
🎯 Strength/Weakness Radar — Radar chart showing relative performance across all subjects
📈 Class Rank & Percentile — "Rank 5 out of 42 students (Top 12%)"
⬆️⬇️ Improvement Indicators — Green/red arrows comparing to previous exam
For Teachers
📋 Class Performance Overview — Average marks per subject, pass/fail counts, at-risk students highlighted in red
📊 Mark Distribution — Histogram showing how many students scored in each range (0-20, 21-40, etc.)
🏆 Top Performers & At-Risk — Quick view of top 5 and bottom 5 students needing attention
For Admin / School Management
🏫 School-wide Performance — Compare class averages across sections (6A vs 6B)
📉 Subject Difficulty Analysis — "Class average in Science is 45% — lowest across all subjects"
AI-Powered (Using Gemini)
Auto-generate parent-friendly progress summaries: "Alice has improved 18% in Mathematics since the last unit test. She continues to excel in English. Consider extra practice in Science where she scored below the class average."
Smart Alerts: Flag students whose performance dropped significantly
